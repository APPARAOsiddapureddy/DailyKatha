01 · The user
A user who has
two minutes,
not twenty.
Profile
Padma, 47
Warangal, Telugu first.
Picked up her first smartphone for WhatsApp four years ago. Sends a "good morning" card to nine groups every day before her tea is cold.
What she does
  • Opens a quotes app, finds a card, holds to download.
  • Switches to WhatsApp, picks Status, posts.
  • Forwards the same card to 4–5 family chats.
What she avoids
  • OTPs, sign-ups, anything English-only.
  • Tiny taps. Bottom sheets that need two reads.
  • Walls of cards she has already seen.

02 · Design principles
Five rules
for every screen.
01
One card forward.
Every screen surfaces a single, ready-to-share card. The rest waits behind one tap.
Home · Section · Feed
02
Native script first.
Telugu, Hindi, Tamil before English. Roman names sit underneath, never above.
Onboarding · Cards
03
Big targets, large text.
Hit areas at 56px, body at 17px. Designed for hands that have done forty years of work.
Buttons · Lists
04
Photo carries the line.
Cards are 9:16 with photo bleed and a serif quote. No frames, no decorative noise.
Card system
05
Login-less, on purpose.
No OTP, no sign-up. Three taps from open to first share. Preferences stay on device.

07 · Live prototype
Walk the whole flow.
Splash auto-advances. Use bottom tabs to jump. Tap a hero card to land in the feed; tap edit to add a photo and caption.
Try this in order
1
Wait for the splash · 1.5s
Auto-advances to language. Pick తెలుగు, then Continue.
2
Pick Hindu (or Spiritual)
Then 2–3 interests on step 3. The button enables when you pick at least one.
3
Land on Home
Tap the Today card → vertical feed → ✎ → Editor. Try changing the photo and adding a caption.
4
Try Explore and You
Festival pack from Explore. Streak and saved cards from You. Edit interests round-trips back to onboarding.
